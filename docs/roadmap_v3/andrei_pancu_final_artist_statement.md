# Andrei Pancu — Final Artist Statement

## Institutional Version
Andrei Pancu works with chemically altered steel plates as active image surfaces. Repeated stamped pattern, oxidation, patina, and other material interventions create a field where structure is present, but never fully stable.[file:4][file:5]

Photography and archival print are central to the practice. They preserve transient states that the plate itself cannot hold, turning a moment of change into a sustained visual form.[file:4][file:5]

The repeated marks function as semiotic fragments: partial signs, coded cues, and visual structures that invite interpretation without fixing meaning in advance. The work is therefore built around the relation between order and instability, not as a theme imposed from outside, but as a condition visible in the material itself.[file:1][file:4]

## Exhibition Version
Andrei Pancu’s work begins with steel plates that are altered, stamped, and exposed to processes of change. The surface is organized, but not allowed to settle; pattern and reaction remain in tension.[file:4][file:5]

Time is part of the work’s structure. The plate continues to change, while photography and archival print preserve selected thresholds that would otherwise pass too quickly to be fully read.[file:4][file:5]

The motifs operate like fragments of a language: not complete statements, but marks that imply relation, sequence, and meaning. That openness is central to the work, which asks the viewer to move between recognition and uncertainty.[file:1][file:4]
