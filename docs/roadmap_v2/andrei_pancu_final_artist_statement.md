# Andrei Pancu — Final Artist Statement

## Institutional Version
Andrei Pancu works with chemically altered steel plates as temporally active image surfaces. Through repeated stamped pattern, oxidation, patina, and other controlled interventions, he develops works in which order is established and then unsettled by ongoing material change.[file:4][file:5]

Photography and archival print are central to the practice. They do not simply document the plates; they preserve transient states the plates themselves cannot hold, allowing a moment of transformation to remain visible and legible.[file:4][file:5]

The repeated motifs function as semiotic fragments rather than fixed symbols. They suggest systems of meaning without resolving them, inviting viewers to construct interpretation through partial recognition, repetition, and instability.[file:1][file:4]

Secondary extensions of the practice include time-lapse video documenting patina progression and small free-standing magnetic metal constructions. These remain related investigations, while the core of the work stays with the steel plates and archival prints.[file:1][file:5]

## Exhibition Version
Andrei Pancu works with steel plates that do not settle. He stamps, alters, and oxidizes their surfaces until pattern and reaction begin to contend with one another, producing images that feel at once deliberate and unstable.[file:4][file:5]

Time is not outside the work but inside it. The plates continue to change, while photography and archival print hold brief thresholds in that process, preserving states that would otherwise pass.[file:4][file:5]

The repeated marks read like fragments of a language that remains incomplete. They point toward structure, memory, and code, yet resist closure, asking the viewer to occupy the space between recognition and uncertainty.[file:1][file:4]
