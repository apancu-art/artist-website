## About-Page Opener
Andrei Pancu is a Montreal-anchored contemporary artist with an operational base in Ottawa. His practice uses chemically altered steel plates, photography, and archival print to create works in which repeated markings, patina, and material change become vehicles for thinking about time, order, instability, and meaning.[file:1][file:2][file:5]

## Portfolio Intro
The core of the work begins with steel plates that remain materially active. Selected states are photographed and translated into archival prints, while related experiments in time-lapse video and magnetic plate constructions extend the same language without displacing the central focus on plates and prints.[file:1][file:4][file:5]
