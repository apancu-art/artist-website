## Long Bio
Andrei Pancu is a contemporary artist anchored in Montreal and working from an operational base in Ottawa. His practice centers on chemically altered steel plates, repeated stamped pattern, photography, and archival print, forming a body of work in which image, surface, and duration remain closely bound.[file:1][file:2]

Pancu works with steel as an active support rather than a neutral ground. Through chemical alteration, oxidation, patina, and repeated marking, he develops surfaces that continue to change over time. Photography and archival printmaking allow selected states within that process to be preserved, producing exhibition-ready works that hold a transient material condition the original plate cannot fully maintain.[file:4][file:5]

Semiotics is central to the work. Repeated motifs operate as partial signs or coded fragments rather than fixed symbols, inviting viewers to move between recognition and uncertainty as meaning is assembled.[file:1][file:4][file:5]

Born in Bucharest and shaped by early childhood in Tel Aviv and later life in Montreal, Pancu works across English, French, Romanian, and Indonesian. He undertook formative Fine Arts study at the Université de Montréal; although incomplete, that period contributed to the development of a mature, self-directed practice grounded in material inquiry and sustained independent work.[file:1]
