## Long Bio
Andrei Pancu is a contemporary artist anchored in Montreal and working from Montreal and Ottawa. His practice centers on chemically altered steel plates, repeated stamped pattern, photography, and archival print, forming a body of work in which surface, time, and interpretation remain closely linked.[file:1][file:2]

Pancu works with steel as an active material rather than a neutral support. Through chemical alteration, oxidation, patina, and repeated marking, he develops surfaces that continue to change after they are made. Photography and archival printing preserve selected thresholds in that process, allowing a transient state to be held in a more stable form without losing its relation to the plate.[file:4][file:5]

The motifs in the work function as semiotic fragments rather than fixed symbols. They suggest a language of partial signs and repeated cues that invite viewers to construct meaning through recognition, interruption, and uncertainty.[file:1][file:4][file:5]

Born in Bucharest, with early childhood in Tel Aviv and later life in Montreal, Pancu works across English, French, Romanian, and Indonesian. He undertook formative Fine Arts study at the Université de Montréal, though the program was not completed; the practice has since developed as a self-directed and sustained body of work grounded in material inquiry.[file:2]
