## Homepage Opener
Andrei Pancu creates work from chemically altered steel plates, repeated stamped pattern, photography, and archival print. His practice is built around time, semiotic fragments, and the unstable relation between material change and preserved image.[file:4][file:5]

## About-Page Opener
Andrei Pancu is a Montreal-anchored artist working from Montreal and Ottawa. His practice uses steel plates as active surfaces, where oxidation, patina, and repeated marking generate images that are held in tension between order and instability.[file:2][file:4][file:5]

## Portfolio Intro
The core of the portfolio is made up of plates and archival prints. Time-lapse video appears only where it clarifies the work’s temporal logic, and small magnetic constructions are presented as experimental satellite forms rather than a separate practice.[file:1][file:5]
