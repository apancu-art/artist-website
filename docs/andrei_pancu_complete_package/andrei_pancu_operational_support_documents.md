# Andrei Pancu — Operational Support Documents


## Plate Dimension Standards
Current development ladder:
- Study / test tier: 6 x 8 in.
- Core production tier: 8 x 10 in.
- Expanded / exhibition tier: 12 x 15 in.

These dimensions preserve a coherent 3:4 to 4:5 family and are preferable to the current 4 x 12 in. format if the narrow strip does not serve the work. Use the 4 x 12 in. plates only as a secondary or experimental format.


## Steel Gauge Standard
Recommended working standard: 16 gauge steel.
Alternative heavier standard: 14 gauge steel for larger or more object-like pieces.
Avoid going thinner than 18 gauge unless a lighter, more delicate plate is specifically desired.

## Pricing Notes
Use stable, series-based pricing rather than ad hoc pricing. Keep the logic clear across work types, and review prices periodically rather than constantly.[file:3]

General guidance:
- Plates: price according to scale, finish, uniqueness, and whether the plate is shown as a source object or as a primary work.
- Prints: set edition structure in advance and keep it consistent.
- Video: define presentation and editioning before pricing.
- Magnetic constructions: keep pricing modest and clear while the form is still experimental.
