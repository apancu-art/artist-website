Revised Artist Bio:
"Andrei Pancu (b. 1968, Bucharest, Romania) is a contemporary artist based in Ottawa, Canada, whose work synthesizes human intervention with natural processes. He is best known for his unique practice of inducing patinas on metal plates, where he orchestrates a dialogue between controlled patterns and the unpredictable forces of oxidation. This method allows him to create textured, abstract works that balance order and chaos, positioning his practice within contemporary dialogues about material process and philosophical inquiry.",
"Pancu's compositions are not static, but active fields of liquid-like flows and subtle color shifts—a direct result of the metal's transformation. Themes of memory, decay, and resilience emerge through the atmospheric surfaces, which often contain fragments of lattice-like structures that evoke an undeciphered language or the ruins of a vanished civilization. His work invites viewers to find beauty in what is broken and unfinished, a reflection of the artist's own exploration into the struggle to impose order on everyday life. Pancu's recent series, including Strange Attractors (2024) and Across the Garden Wall (2025), exemplify his ongoing investigation into the interaction of opposing forces.",
"Andrei Pancu studied Fine Arts at the Université de Montréal. His works are held in private collections and have been exhibited across Canada."
