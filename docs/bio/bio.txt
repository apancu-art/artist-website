
Andrei Pancu (b. 1968, Bucharest, Romania) is an Ottawa-based contemporary artist whose practice centers on inducing patination reactions on metal plates. He engages a meticulous process of impressing deterministic patterns and masks onto dynamically growing patinas, creating vibrant, amorphous fields of interference subtly colored by the oxidation of the metal substrate. This unique method allows Pancu to explore the profound dialogue between human will and natural entropy, reflecting on themes of order, chaos, and synthesis, drawing inspiration from philosophers such as Hegel.

For Pancu, the surface of the metal plate functions as a darkroom plate where the image develops. He meticulously photographs the ephemeral progression of the patina throughout its development, capturing fleeting states from which he creates limited-edition fine art prints. His works feature fragments of lattice-like structures that form a cryptic visual language, inviting viewers into an active inquiry into surface, memory, and perception, to discover meaning within these evolving, coded landscapes.

Andrei Pancu studied Fine Arts at the Université de Montréal.
