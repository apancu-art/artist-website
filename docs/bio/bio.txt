
BIO

Andrei Pancu (b. 1968, Bucharest, Romania) is a contemporary artist based in Ottawa, Canada, whose work explores the intersection of human intervention and natural processes. By chemically inducing patinas on metal plates and stamping them with patterns of recurring motifs, he develops vibrant fields of interference colored by the oxidation of the metal surface. Pancu's distinctive method is inspired by Hegel's theory of synthesis, as he explores the profound dialogue between order and chaos. With the metal substrate serving as the darkroom plate on which the image develops, Pancu captures ephemeral states throughout its progression, from which he creates limited-edition prints.
Themes of memory, decay, and resilience emerge through the atmospheric composition of Pancu's works. Fragments of lattice-like symbols evoke an undeciphered language, inviting the viewer to find meaning in what is broken and unfinished, a reflection of the artist's own exploration into the struggle to impose order on everyday life. Pancu's recent series, including Strange Attractors (2024) and Across the Garden Wall (2025), exemplify his ongoing investigation into the creative power of opposing forces.
Andrei Pancu studied Fine Arts at the Université de Montréal. His works are held in private collections.
