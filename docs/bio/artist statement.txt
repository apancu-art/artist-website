
ARTIST STATEMENT

I work on metal plate, inducing patinas to develop across the surface which I then stamp with intricate patterns. In this process, controlled marks meet unpredictable natural processes, creating vibrant areas of interference. The metal responds with subtle shifts of color, earthy reds, greens, and blues that emerge from the oxidation itself, creating ethereal scenes and landscapes that are revealed rather than made. The recurring motifs I press into the surface look like the remnants of a lost language, engaging the viewer in a hunt for meaning.
What drives me is the dialogue between human intervention and natural forces, the inherent tension transforming its component parts into something entirely new. The process is inspired by Hegel's theory of synthesis, with my works developing organically rather than being built up, each piece capturing a moment of becoming, where metal, chemistry, and time converge into something neither fully controlled nor entirely accidental.
Through the mystery of the unfinished and the uncertain, I aim to reawaken a sense of wonder in what is transient and ephemeral. Capturing fleeting states of material becoming, I invite contemplation of memory, surface, and time, and the enduring beauty found in uncertainty.
