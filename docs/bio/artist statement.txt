

I am a contemporary artist composing on metal plates by impressing patterns onto chemically-induced patinas. The interplay of the static, deterministic stamp pattern with the dynamic, stochastic growth of the patina creates vibrant, amorphous areas of interference. The entire surface is subtly colored by the oxidation of the metal substrate. It is within these evolving, coded landscapes that my work unfolds, inviting contemplation on the collaboration between intention and unpredictability. It is a process of creation not through accumulation, but of development through transformation and synthesis.
My fascination with the natural world's patterns and their interaction with projections of my own imagination has always driven my artistic exploration. To engage the viewer in this dialogue, I create patterns composed of symbols of unknown languages, to evoke the subconscious into contributing to the hunt for meaning. Through this dialogue between control and chaos, my work seeks to evoke an ancient mystery, to rekindle a forgotten sense of innocent awe and wonder.
