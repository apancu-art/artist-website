Andrei Pancu is a contemporary artist born in Bucharest, Romania, and based in Montreal and Ottawa. His practice uses chemically altered steel plates, stamped pattern, photography, and print to explore surfaces that change over time. In his work, repeating motifs function as semiotic fragments: signs that invite viewers to construct meaning while material change keeps the image unsettled. Pancu also develops related time-based and sculptural studies, including time-lapse documentation of patina progression and small magnetic plate constructions. He studied Fine Arts at the Université de Montréal.
